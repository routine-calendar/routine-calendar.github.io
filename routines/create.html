How to add it

  1. Install Routine Calendar on your Android phone.
  2. Tap Copy link above (on a computer, send the link to your phone first).
  3. In the app, open Create routine, expand Import shared routine, paste the link and tap Import.
  4. Check the routine and tap Save.
